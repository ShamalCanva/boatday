Drop your harbour/sky photos in this folder with EXACTLY these filenames:

  clear.jpg
  cloudy.jpg
  rain.jpg
  sunset.jpg
  night.jpg

The Plan tab picks one automatically based on live weather + time of day.
If a file is missing, the site quietly falls back to a generated gradient
in the same mood — nothing breaks either way.

Tips for good source photos:
- Landscape orientation, at least 1200px wide
- Avoid photos with a lot of fine detail near the top third of the frame —
  that's where the "BOAT DAY" heading text sits
- A darker/moodier shot works better than a bright one; the app already
  darkens photos slightly so white text stays readable over them
